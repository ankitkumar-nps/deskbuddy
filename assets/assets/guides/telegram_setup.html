💬 Telegram Chat Setup

Send messages from your phone to a friend's DeskBuddy via Telegram relay.

1

Tap "Activate DeskBuddy Chat"

This opens Telegram with the DeskBuddy bot. If Telegram isn't installed, install it first from the Play Store.

2

Send /start in Telegram

Once Telegram opens, just tap the Start button or send /start. Then come back to the DeskBuddy app.

3

Wait for verification

The app polls Telegram every 5 seconds. Within 15 seconds it should say Chat Ready ✓. If it times out, tap Activate again.

4

Share your Device ID

Tap the QR code icon to show your Device ID (looks like DB_A1B2C3D4). Share this with the friend you want to chat with.

5

Add a contact

Tap Add Contact, enter your friend's Device ID and give them a name. Now you can message each other's DeskBuddies!

⚡ How it works

Messages are relayed privately through the DeskBuddy Telegram bot. Your contacts only see the DeskBuddy app — not your Telegram account. Messages appear on your friend's OLED screen directly via BLE.

🔒 Privacy

The bot only stores your Device ID and chat ID. No personal data, no message history is saved on any server. Messages are deleted from Telegram after delivery.
